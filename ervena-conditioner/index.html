Full ingredients (INCI)

Aqua, Cetearyl Alcohol, Distearoylethyl Dimonium Chloride (or Varisoft equivalent conditioning agent), Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Glycerin, Butyrospermum Parkii Butter, Chamomilla Recutita Flower Extract, Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate, Decyl Glucoside, Argania Spinosa Kernel Oil, Simmondsia Chinensis Seed Oil, Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Extract, Hydroxypropyl Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride (Goma Guar Quaternizada), Panthenol, Tocopherol, Citrus Reticulata Peel Oil, Elettaria Cardamomum Seed Oil, Hibiscus Sabdariffa Flower Extract, Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract, Citric Acid, Limonene*, Linalool*, Citral*, Geraniol*, Citronellol*.

*Naturally occurring components in essential oils and botanical extracts.

Directions

After cleansing with Ervena Shampoo, apply evenly to damp hair from mid-lengths to ends. Leave on for 2 to 3 minutes to allow deep conditioning, then rinse thoroughly.

Warnings & allergy alert

Warnings: For external use only. Keep out of reach of children. Avoid contact with eyes; if contact occurs, rinse immediately with plenty of water. Discontinue use if irritation or redness develops.

Allergy alert: Contains natural plant extracts and essential oils. Perform a patch test 24 hours prior to first use.
